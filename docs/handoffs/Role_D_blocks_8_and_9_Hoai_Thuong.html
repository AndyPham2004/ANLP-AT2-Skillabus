Body copy, 60 words:

Each pipeline stage pits a traditional baseline against a modern method. Extraction is scored by F1; matching, a ranking task, by Precision@5; open-ended chat by an LLM-as-judge. A measured baseline already justifies the comparison: exact ESCO matching covers only 36% of our 133-skill dictionary, and 55% — including the whole LLM stack — have no ESCO concept at all.
Caption: Baseline from Role B's data-eda (133-skill dictionary vs ESCO); all metrics on held-out, labelled data with test-set n shown per task.
Source of the baseline number: taken from Role B's handoff ("For D, block 8 — a measured baseline"): exact label matching maps 36% of the 133-skill dictionary onto ESCO; 73 skills (55%) have no ESCO concept (Docker, Kubernetes, XGBoost, Snowflake, Tableau, and the LLM stack). This is the number the semantic matcher must beat.
