Skillabus closes the gap between your degree and the job ad
We put subject outlines, job ads and resumes into one skills vocabulary, so a student sees which skills a role needs that they lack, and which subject fills each gap.

1The problem

Subject outlines speak academic. Job ads speak industry. Nothing maps one to the other.

“Implement and evaluate advanced machine learning techniques to real-world problems.”
UTS Handbook 2026, 36120, learning objective 1
“In-depth knowledge of deep learning, Generative AI, and other advanced AI technologies … Experience in building and deploying AI models and solutions.”
Robert Walters ad, Data Scientist – Python, SQL, Sydney CBD, 23 Sep 2026

Same skill, different words. Only the ad asks for generative AI, which ESCO has no concept for yet.

2Three users, one question each
Elective chooser
Which subject moves me closest to my target role?
Job seeker
What does my resume lack for this ad?
Career switcher
How far is my profile from a new role?
Expected outcome: in under a minute, a ranked shortlist of electives that close the gap and a resume that knows what it lacks.
